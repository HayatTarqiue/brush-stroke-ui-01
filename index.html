Ask a Question
What areas do you serve?

We work Pan India — covering residential, commercial and industrial projects. Recent work spans Bengaluru, Indore, Goa, Pune and Vadodara.

How long does a typical interior project take?

Depending on scope, most interior projects are completed within 45–90 days. Turnkey projects may take 4–8 months depending on scale.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes! We provide a complimentary initial consultation to understand your requirements and provide a detailed proposal.

What design styles do you specialize in?

We work across all major styles — Modern, Minimalist, Classical, Scandinavian, Industrial, Japandi, Bohemian, Parisian, Art Deco, and more.

Is there a minimum project size?

We typically work on projects starting from 500 sq ft for interiors and 1000 sq ft for civil construction.

Do you provide warranties?

Yes, we offer comprehensive warranties on all our work — typically 1–10 years depending on the component.
